Swiping Privacy Policy

Effective 3 November 2026 · Swiping is a mobile game made by Ovheda

The short version: Swiping has no accounts and no servers of its own. Your progress stays on your phone. The game shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google may use your phone's advertising ID only in the ways you allow. Buying Remove Ads goes through Apple or Google Play, not through us.

Who we are

Swiping is published by Ovheda ("we", "us"). If you have any question about this policy or your information, email business@ovheda.com.

What the game keeps on your phone

To remember where you are, Swiping saves a few things on your device only:

  • your coins, upgrade levels, and the skins and weapons you own or wear
  • your sound setting
  • whether you've seen the first-launch screen
  • how many runs you've played since the last ad, and how many optional ads you've watched for shop items
  • whether you've bought Remove Ads

We never receive this information. It stays on your phone and is deleted when you uninstall the game.

What we don't do

  • We don't ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • We don't have accounts, chat, or any way for players to message each other.
  • We don't use analytics, attribution or tracking tools of our own, and we don't sell your information.

Ads (Google AdMob)

Swiping is free and is paid for by ads from Google AdMob: a short ad after some runs, and optional ads you can choose to watch for a reward (for example, double coins or a revive).

To show and measure ads, Google's ad software in the game may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier (the IDFA on iPhone, the advertising ID on Android), your IP address, your device type and settings, and how you interact with the ads. Google uses this to show ads, limit how often you see the same ad, prevent fraud and measure ad performance, as described in its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services

Your choices:

  • In Europe, the UK and other places where the law requires it, Google's consent form appears the first time you open the game, and you decide whether ads may be personalised. You can change your choice any time in the game: tap the gear on the start screen, then Privacy settings.
  • On iPhone, Apple asks whether Swiping may use your advertising identifier ("Allow Tracking"). You'll see the same number of ads either way; only how well they fit you changes. You can change this in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (usually Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Remove Ads purchase

If you buy Remove Ads, the payment is handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your payment details. The game only receives confirmation that the purchase went through, and saves "Remove Ads owned" on your phone. You can restore the purchase from the Remove Ads screen on iPhone. Apple's and Google's own privacy policies cover the payment.

The game engine

Swiping is made with the Unity engine and uses Unity's in-app purchase library to talk to the App Store and Google Play. We don't use Unity's analytics or other online Unity services. Unity's own policy is at unity.com/legal/privacy-policy.

Children

Swiping is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you are a parent or guardian and think your child has given information through the game, email us and we'll help.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or the UK GDPR), you may have the right to ask what information is held about you, to have it deleted or corrected, to withdraw your consent to personalised ads, or to complain to your local data protection authority. Because the game's own data stays on your phone and we don't receive it, deleting the game removes it. Information held by Google about ads is managed through Google's controls linked above. For anything else, email business@ovheda.com and we'll reply as soon as we can, and within one month where the law requires it.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Swiping handles information, we'll update this page and its effective date.
